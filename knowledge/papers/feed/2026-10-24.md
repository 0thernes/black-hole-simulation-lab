## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Strong Field Gravitational Lensing in Full Wave Optics** — J. Sieiro dos
  Santos and L. Vujeva, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Computes full
  wave-optics amplification factors, with a perturbative framework, for
  gravitational waves from a stellar-mass binary lensed by a nearby massive
  (Schwarzschild or Kerr) black hole, where geometric-optics and thin-lens
  assumptions fail. Edge-on binaries peak off perfect alignment, because their
  quadrupolar emission breaks axisymmetry. Strong-field ray tracing agrees to
  about 10% for face-on Schwarzschild lensing; a Kerr lens matches its
  Schwarzschild counterpart to similar accuracy once frame dragging is
  corrected for, while a point-mass lens fails. A wave-optics cross-check
  for the renderer's geometric-optics Kerr lensing. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18958
- **Black Hole Shadows and Quasinormal Modes with Dark Matter at the
  Horizon** — A. Sadeghi et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). A
  Schwarzschild black hole inside a Hernquist halo, as a one-parameter family
  at fixed halo mass that varies how the density vanishes at the horizon;
  regularity needs at least linear vanishing, and the dominant energy
  condition fails in a thin layer outside the horizon. At fixed black-hole
  mass the halo always enlarges the shadow. Against EHT Sgr A\*/M87\* only
  compact halos (within a few tens of gravitational radii) are constrained,
  and the bounds depend on whether the measured mass is taken as the hole's
  or the total. The halo lowers QNM frequencies and damping, consistent with
  the eikonal photon-sphere link. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30312
- **Optical constraints on rotating logarithmic- and exponential-entropy
  wormholes from strong-field lensing** — A. Errehymy et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). For slowly rotating traversable wormholes with an
  entropy-motivated lapse, derives the photon sphere, strong-deflection
  coefficients and Lyapunov exponent in closed form. Shadow diameters can be
  tuned to within 5–11% of Kerr, so the shadow alone cannot tell them apart.
  A five-component fingerprint (rotational asymmetry, two lensing
  coefficients, Lyapunov exponent, orbital frequency) differs from Kerr by at
  least 5% in each component, giving a combined signal-to-noise of about 3.8
  at an assumed 10% measurement level (the authors' estimate). Exotic matter
  required. *tier: preprint / speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25132

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Scalar wave dark matter imprints on binary black hole mergers:
  dependence on field mass and density** — Y.-P. Zhang et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Full NR simulations of nonspinning binaries (q = 1–4)
  inside complex scalar-field clouds, with initial data that hold the energy
  density fixed while the field mass varies. The environment speeds up the
  merger and suppresses radiated energy and angular momentum; differential
  accretion shifts the horizon mass ratio before merger; gravitational plus
  scalar linear momentum can raise the kick to about 2064 km/s (q = 2, dense,
  massless field) against 138 km/s in vacuum. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15053
- **Gravitational waves from highly charged binary black holes in
  Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton theory** — R. Tomie et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Nonlinear NR mergers of charged binaries for several
  dilaton couplings a. Weak charges leave the dominant waveform near the GR
  baseline. At high, opposite charges, waveforms visibly differ between a ≠ 0
  and pure Einstein–Maxwell, and at a = 1 the time to merger from a fixed
  separation is about 62% shorter than for the neutral binary. Like-charged
  binaries merge 15–20% later and leave charged remnants with secondary
  dilaton hair; opposite-charge remnants approach neutral, hairless holes.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31177
- **Broken isospectrality in inverted black hole potentials and the
  emergence of Efimov scaling** — U. Shahzad and S. H. Völkel, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Gives the first direct computation of the bound-state
  spectrum of the inverted Zerilli potential used in Mashhoon's approach to
  QNMs. Although Regge–Wheeler and Zerilli are isospectral for QNMs, their
  inverted forms have distinct bound-state spectra, and inversion breaks the
  first-order Darboux relation. Both spectra still show Efimov-like geometric
  scaling. The authors say this raises questions for recent methods that
  compute QNMs precisely from numerical bound states, which matters for
  ringdown fitting. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27102
- **Spinformation: do gravitational-wave events carry information beyond
  effective spins?** — C. Plunkett et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc).
  Measures how much component-spin information each GWTC-5 event carries
  beyond χ_eff and χ_p. With an information-loss threshold of about 0.25
  bits, over 80% of events are described by χ_eff alone and only about 3%
  carry substantial extra spin information; simulations show detector noise
  alone can create or destroy over a bit, so the remaining structure may be
  noise-driven. Relevant when setting spin priors for rendered remnants.
  *tier: preprint / observational_constraint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18973

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Perturbations and phenomenology of compact objects in quantum gravity
  theories** — A. Spina, 2026, *PhD thesis* (gr-qc; arXiv comment gives the
  defense date as 2026-10-14). Covers Einstein–Weyl quadratic gravity
  (Gregory–Laflamme-type monopole instabilities, massive-field tails, a
  breakdown of the eikonal QNM–null-geodesic correspondence, and horizonless
  "veiled singularities" that can mimic shadows), then a dynamical regular
  black hole from collapse in asymptotically safe gravity. That model's
  shadow stays within current constraints, while evaporation runs cooler and
  slower. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31536
- **Holographic Pseudo Entropy from the Rindler Method in AdS and Flat
  Space** — M. Hakami Shalamzari et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (hep-th).
  Uses the Rindler map to a rotating BTZ black hole to identify the timelike
  extremal curve behind holographic timelike entanglement entropy (pseudo
  entropy) in AdS₃/CFT₂: a geodesic stretched between the inner and outer
  horizons, of length iπℓ for any interval, which alone gives the imaginary
  part iπc/6. The flat₃/CCFT₂ analogue lies between the cosmological horizon
  and timelike infinity. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17239
- **Principle of no paradoxical Universe** — N. P. D. Loc, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (hep-ph). **Fringe / conjectural.** If dark energy is dynamical
  (as recent DESI results may suggest) and the Universe recrunches in finite
  time, sufficiently massive black holes never finish evaporating. The author
  conjectures that the Universe avoids configurations where the information
  paradox is actually realized, and derives a lower black-hole mass bound as a
  function of cosmic lifetime. Depends on a contested cosmological reading.
  *tier: speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23606
