## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Spin inference with the Black Hole Explorer. I. Fisher information matrix
  forecast** — J. Farah et al., 2026, *preprint*. Fisher-matrix forecast with a
  dual-cone semi-analytic emission model. It finds that BHEX photon-ring data
  could constrain the dimensionless spin of M87* and Sgr A* to σ_a* ≪ 0.1 after
  30 orbits, even with significant systematics, and cross-checks the result
  against full posterior sampling. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/79f67c572baa50a4a957c78626248a97/
- **Dynamics and Spectra-Polarimetric Signatures of GRMHD Simulations with
  Multiple Magnetic Loops** — R. Kinadeter et al., 2026, *preprint*. 3D GRMHD
  with alternating-polarity poloidal loops. Jet power is strongly variable while
  the loops accrete, then settles to a semi-MAD state. The jet emission is
  depolarized by Faraday rotation and conversion, and the disk emission matches
  Sgr A* quiescent constraints from radio to X-ray. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/f197d119c7d75a75b2428645c3576455/
- **Video reconstruction of variable VLBI observations with neural fields** —
  M. Foschi et al., 2026, *Nature*. `kine` reconstructs time-continuous
  polarimetric videos from VLBI data with a neural-field representation. It is
  demonstrated on multi-epoch VLBA observations of 3C 345 and targets horizon-scale
  Sgr A* EHT videos. This is a relevant reference for comparing time-resolved
  renders. *tier: observational_constraint (method).*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/1c30bc4c9c405ddfa38e5770e86c6ea2/
- **VAPOLA II: Spectropolarimetric properties of AGNs and Sgr A* with ALMA,
  2017–2023** — D. Carlos et al., 2026, *preprint*. Covers 39 AGN plus Sgr A*
  across five EHT-era VLBI campaigns. Polarization fraction, EVPA and rotation
  measure vary day to day, and |RM| rises with frequency. A Faraday sheath fits
  the data, but an accretion-flow origin for Sgr A* is not ruled out. *tier:
  observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/eda30ca5155358828a7a418ff757c28a/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Vishveshwara's waveform revisited: insights from a Keldysh quasinormal mode
  expansion** — J. Besson et al., 2026, *preprint*. Hyperboloidal, bi-orthogonal
  QNM expansion of Schwarzschild scattering. The second waveform peak tracks the
  fundamental mode plus the first overtones. For Zerilli perturbations only, the
  first peak is controlled by the algebraically special mode and the branch cut.
  This bears on ringdown start-time choices. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/4e72b26610a25fddac295b4c2ed65d54/
- **Modeling the merger-ringdown of an eccentric test-mass inspiral into a Kerr
  black hole using the effective-one-body framework** — (authors not listed in
  index), 2026, *Physical Review D*. EOB trajectories with a Teukolsky source,
  spanning a ∈ [−0.9, 0.9] and e_LSO ≤ 0.9. Eccentricity mainly shapes the last
  peak and barely affects the ringdown. The paper builds a merger-ringdown model
  with QNM mode mixing. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/aedb3035d5eb5babbe844eccfc5713e2/
- **Low-lying quasinormal modes of Kazakov–Solodukhin black holes from Chebyshev
  and continued-fraction methods** — A. H. Al-Yoorby, 2026, *Classical and
  Quantum Gravity*. Uses cross-validated Chebyshev collocation and a Leaver
  continued fraction. The Schwarzschild-limit benchmark agrees to ~10⁻¹¹–10⁻¹³,
  a useful acceptance recipe for any QNM solver. The deformed-metric shifts
  themselves are beyond GR. *tier: numerical_approximation (method) /
  speculative_extension (KS metric).*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/c34f1c05bcec597d987fbab554de2ea7/
- **A catalogue of insights from the fourth LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA observing run** —
  C. P. L. Berry et al., 2026, *review preprint*. Summarizes O4: ~100 new
  detections per catalogue, the loudest signals so far, evidence for multiple
  subpopulations, and population-level GR tests. The final O4 catalogue is due in
  December 2026. *tier: observational_constraint / review.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/c5aa4233cc9355f287ac2ef9a22e7dce/
- **Testing the spin-induced multipole moments of compact binary coalescences
  using the flexible theory-independent framework** — E. M. Sänger et al., 2026,
  *preprint*. A parameterized inspiral test of the no-hair quadrupole and octupole,
  applied to LVK events. It forecasts O(10⁻²) and O(10⁻¹) constraints for
  ET/CE, two orders of magnitude tighter than today. *tier: preprint /
  observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/11280d1d9d24589093be5221924e4778/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Dark Matter Signatures in Black Hole Thermodynamics and Information
  Recovery** — Y. Ladghami et al., 2026, *preprint*. Schwarzschild and RN black
  holes in perfect-fluid dark matter. The island formula yields a Page curve that
  saturates at 2S_BH, and the dark matter shortens both the Page time and the
  lifetime. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/903c5739c3655b84a969d0bb87f528dd/
- **Quantum-corrected thermodynamics of Schwarzschild AdS black holes in
  conformal Killing gravity** — S. Soroushfar et al., 2026, *preprint*.
  Non-perturbative entropy corrections shift the phase transitions at small r_H.
  The island prescription gives S_sat = 2(πr_H² + η e^(−πr_H²)). The paper
  reports that η cancels from the extremal universal relation. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/e0a94b3a297a5c31a3cfe7e1a8820f05/
