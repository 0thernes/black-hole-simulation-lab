## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **SpinOctopus: A Versatile, User-Friendly, and Extensible Public Code for
  General-Relativistic Ray-Tracing in axisymmetric spacetime** — S. Hu et al.,
  2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Public Fortran/Python ray tracer with OpenMP
  for arbitrary axisymmetric metrics: the user supplies only the covariant
  metric and a configuration file. Handles timelike orbits and light curves,
  null geodesics, and images of thin, thick and tilted disks, jets, spherical
  accretion and point sources, with an adaptive step-size integrator; the
  authors report about 800 rays per second per thread on an Intel i7-14700 for
  thick-disk images (their benchmark). Fe Kα line profiles and thick tilted
  disks are deferred to later releases. A possible cross-check reference for
  the renderer's geodesic integrator. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29800
- **An end-to-end function for the photograph of a Schwarzschild black hole** —
  P. Li et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Writes the map from a point
  source to its image point on a tangent image plane (the "photograph
  function") in closed form for Schwarzschild, for sources on the celestial
  sphere and in the equatorial plane, without integrating the intermediate null
  geodesics. The authors call the construction exact; it exposes the
  multivalued branch structure, the link to the photon sphere and shadow edge,
  and the self-similar higher-order images. A candidate analytic test oracle
  for a Schwarzschild ray tracer, not yet checked against one here. *tier:
  preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29437
- **Resistive alignment of a black-hole magnetosphere** — R. Lier and
  O. Porth, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE). Analytic theory, within
  resistive relativistic MHD, of why simulated magnetospheres rapidly align the
  magnetic and spin axes: starting from the slow-spin Blandford–Znajek monopole,
  the induction equation reduces to a relaxation equation for the current-sheet
  inclination, and alignment comes from second-order spin terms combined with
  explicit resistive dissipation. The predicted late-time decay is compared with
  resistive-GRMHD runs at several spins and resistivities. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13288
- **Linear Response to Black Hole Deformations** — S. Hohenegger, 2026,
  *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Proposes a linear-response formalism for the
  leading corrections to Schwarzschild observables anywhere outside the
  horizon, with the order fixed by an eigenvalue of the Einstein tensor read as
  an external stress-energy source. Illustrated for static, spherically
  symmetric cases on the shadow size, ISCO radius and scalar QNM spectrum, and
  inverted to argue that such observables constrain the effective new physics.
  Spherical, static only; no Kerr extension yet. *tier: preprint /
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18521

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **How general relativity stacks up: Testing gravity by coherently combining
  gravitational-wave observations** — L. Passenger et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Population-level test of GR that assumes deviations are
  strongest near merger, vary smoothly with total mass and are dominated by the
  ℓ=|m|=2 mode, then combines events coherently. Applied to 28 high-SNR binary
  black hole mergers from the LVK GWTC-5 catalog, it finds no evidence for
  deviations from GR; the authors argue that the mass-dependent reconstruction
  can separate waveform systematics from genuine violations. The null result
  holds only within these stated assumptions. *tier: preprint /
  observational_constraint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29247
- **Exceptional points and hysteresis in Kerr-Newman quasi-normal modes** —
  M. Misas Arcos et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Hyperboloidal
  formulation of the coupled gravito-electromagnetic Teukolsky equations, solved
  with a Chebyshev pseudospectral method for the ℓ=m=2 spectrum. Finds four
  exceptional points intrinsic to Kerr–Newman (previously seen in Kerr only with
  an auxiliary scalar field), up to 99.974% of extremality, with square-root
  gap closing and mode exchange (hysteresis) under quasistatic loops around
  them. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30049
- **Modeling QNMs-Filtered Gravitational Waveforms: Approximation Limits and
  Filtering Effects** — J.-X. Shi et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc).
  Examines the "direct wave" left after QNM filtering of binary black hole
  waveforms. Point-particle models disagree noticeably with filtered BBH
  waveforms; stationary-phase particle templates work reasonably for (2,2) but
  poorly for (4,4). Although the filter is all-pass it materially changes the
  modeling, and Schwarzschild perturbations from Gaussian initial data can
  produce direct-wave-like signals after filtering, depending on where the data
  sit relative to the light ring. Contributes to an open debate. *tier:
  preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30031
- **Strong-Field Gravitational-Wave Lensing from Ray-Traced Arrival-Time
  Surfaces** — L. Vujeva et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Builds the
  time-delay surface for a binary lensed by a massive black hole from the
  Hamilton–Jacobi structure of null geodesics, dropping the thin-lens
  approximation, and solves the diffraction integral on it. For a stationary
  source at 10M it matches a relativistic wave-scattering model to 6.5% in peak
  amplification and 0.3% in the first fringe, and agrees with the point-mass
  lens to <1% in amplitude at 10⁴M (with high-frequency dephasing); claimed
  valid from about 6M to infinity. Companion: Sieiro dos Santos & Vujeva,
  arXiv:2610.18958 (full wave optics, not listed separately). *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18984

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Grey-body factors of black holes** — R. Konoplya and A. Zhidenko, 2026,
  *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc; arXiv comment says invited review for Reports on
  Progress in Physics). Unified review of transmission through black-hole
  potentials in flat, de Sitter and AdS settings: links to Hawking spectra,
  absorption cross sections and ringdown, computational methods, the
  grey-body/QNM correspondence and its stability under potential deformations,
  wormholes, superradiance, modified gravity, and absorption probabilities as
  boundary correlators in gauge/gravity duality. *tier: preprint / review
  (numerical_approximation for the semiclassical core).*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24356
- **Codimension-Zero Holographic Complexity and Spectral Three-Point
  Correlators** — M. Miyaji et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (hep-th). In the
  complexity=anything framework, codimension-zero complexity is governed by
  three-point correlators of the density of states, tying it to Euclidean
  wormholes; it reduces to two codimension-one pieces with exponentially long
  linear growth followed by saturation at the Heisenberg time, the plateau being
  set by spectral level repulsion. Also constructs complexity operators from an
  orthonormalized overcomplete basis. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13289
- **Semiclassical effects on ultramassive spacetimes** — V. Boyanov et al.,
  2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Follows Senovilla's "ultramassive" scenario,
  where accretion pushes a Schwarzschild–de Sitter black hole past horizon
  coalescence into a collapsing Kantowski–Sachs-like universe with no future
  infinity or event horizon. Studies Hawking radiation and the renormalized
  stress-energy tensor there in the Polyakov (2D) approximation, and the
  backreaction on horizon evolution near extremality and through the
  subsequent collapse. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29857
- **Quantum critical collapse in the large D limit** — C.-H. Wu, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Shows the s-wave quantum self-energy growing mode in
  Einstein–scalar critical collapse keeps its universal Lyapunov exponent D−2
  for both continuously and discretely self-similar solutions at large D. In
  CSS a "quantum wall" forms at r_h ∝ (D−2)⁻², above the Planck scale at
  finite D; in DSS a covariant one-loop action is built whose stress tensor is
  smooth and echo-periodic, suggesting the same for 4D Choptuik. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20705
