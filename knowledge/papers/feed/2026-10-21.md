## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Autocorrelation in black hole flare movies: Departures from Kerr** — F.-S. Wei
  et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE; gr-qc cross-list). Time-angle
  autocorrelation of ray-traced movies of an orbiting hot spot in the
  Johannsen–Psaltis metric (one deformation parameter) encodes the time delays
  and angular separations of lensed images. The slope k and intercept b of an
  approximately linear secondary ridge trace distinctive paths in the (k, b)
  plane as spin and deformation vary, so the pair can jointly constrain both
  once orbital radius, inclination and angular velocity are specified. Sweeping
  the orbital angular velocity defines a Kerr reference region, and some
  low-spin, negative-deformation configurations land outside it for sub-,
  super- and Keplerian motion. Idealized hot-spot forecast, not data.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12199
- **FUGUE: A New GPU-accelerated General Relativistic Magnetohydrodynamics Code
  with Weak-Field Multipole Self-Gravity and Quasi-Stationary Kerr Black Hole
  Evolution** — P. Plonka and A. Janiuk, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE;
  arXiv comment says in press in ApJ Supplement Series). GRMHD code with
  shock-capturing and RK2 time integration, an optional quasi-stationary update
  of the Kerr mass and spin, and a spherical-harmonic Poisson solver for
  weak-field multipole self-gravity. Validated with hydrodynamic, MHD,
  general-relativistic and self-gravity convergence tests, then applied to a
  self-gravitating, Toomre-unstable, magnetized 1 M☉ disk around a Kerr black
  hole, where gravitational instabilities accompany changes in the accretion
  rate and Blandford–Znajek luminosity. The authors report over 470 million
  zone-cycles per second on one NVIDIA GH200 GPU, which they say equals or
  exceeds current state-of-the-art GRMHD codes (their claim). Thematic
  neighbor of the GRMHD imaging entries in 2026-09-29 and 2026-10-14.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20869
- **Optical Signatures of a Black Hole in Effective Quantum Gravity** — J. Khasanov
  et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Weak lensing and shadow of a black hole
  in effective quantum gravity surrounded by uniform and non-uniform plasma:
  shadow radii shrink with the black-hole charge and with both plasma
  parameters, and the deflection angle rises with uniform plasma frequency and
  falls for non-uniform plasma. Uses EHT data to bound the spacetime parameters;
  the bounds hold only for this metric and these plasma models. *tier: preprint /
  observational_constraint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16721
- **X-ray Comptonization in an Accreting Kerr Black Hole System with a Tilted Disc
  and Thermal Corona; A General-Relativistic Monte Carlo Simulation: I. Model,
  Radiative Transfer and Spectral-Timing Properties** — M. Chatterjee and
  D. Bhattacharya, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE). Paper I of two: a
  general-relativistic Monte Carlo radiative-transfer model of a misaligned,
  warped, two-zone disc–corona system around a Kerr black hole, with geodesic
  integration, coronal Compton scattering and outer-disc reflection, built to
  test the Lense–Thirring precession picture of QPOs. Reports spectra, light
  curves and spectral lags against mass, corona size, outer-disc geometry,
  albedo, injection radius and inclination, and finds that light bending
  systematically lengthens photon path-length and arrival-time distributions.
  Polarization is deferred to Paper II. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26769

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Binary Black Hole Puncture Initial Data with Physics-Informed Neural Networks**
  — T. Assumpção et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Pinndorama, a PINN code
  that solves the Hamiltonian constraint in the puncture formalism, trained only
  on the equation and boundary conditions (Adam, then self-scaled Broyden), with
  no guiding solution. Tested on a spherical case with an exact solution,
  axisymmetric unequal-spin punctures and a fully 3D GW150914-like configuration;
  relative L² errors against NRPyElliptic are about 10⁻⁸ in axisymmetry and 10⁻⁵
  in 3D. A parametric network that takes the puncture spin as input also cuts
  NRPyElliptic's hyperbolic-relaxation time by about 4.6×. Presented as a
  complement to conventional elliptic solvers. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27094
- **Large mass-ratio (3 < q ≤ 10) inspiral-merger-ringdown model for eccentric
  binary black holes** — P. Manna et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc).
  EccHybIMR22, a fully analytical (2,2)-mode model made by smoothly attaching an
  eccentric inspiral to a quasi-circular merger-ringdown and trained on
  eccentric NR with q ≤ 10. Uses the expanded SXS catalog to build 91 PN–NR
  hybrids reaching down to 20 M☉ at 20 Hz, with a little under a third as
  training targets. On the rest it recovers 65% of hybrids with mismatches
  ≲ 3% at M = 40 M☉ for e₂₀Hz ≲ 0.33 (q ≤ 3) or ≲ 0.12 (q > 3), and against 175
  SXS simulations it recovers nearly 80% for e ≲ 0.3. Same problem area as the eccentric-model entries in 2026-07-08 and
  the eccentric/precessing catalog in 2026-10-15. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16958
- **Search for Compact Binary Coalescences with Sub-solar Mass Components in Data
  from the Second Part of LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA's Fourth Observing Run** —
  LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA Collaboration, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc; astro-ph.HE).
  Searches 2024-04-10 to 2025-01-28 data for secondary masses 0.2–1 M☉ (primary
  0.2–10 M☉). Two of three pipelines show outlier candidates, none significant
  enough to claim a detection. Sets 90% merger-rate upper limits for sub-solar
  black holes of 50–5000 Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹ depending on masses, limits the dark-matter
  fraction in primordial black holes to ≤ 0.018–0.032 at 1 M☉ (early-forming
  binaries), and gives 249 Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹ for neutron-star binaries with a sub-solar
  component under a fixed population model. A null result with model-dependent
  dark-matter interpretations. *tier: preprint / observational_constraint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20276
- **Curvature Reconstruction and Tensor Polarizations in Isospectrality-Breaking
  Black-Hole Perturbations** — P. Wagle et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc).
  For nonrotating black holes in dynamical Chern–Simons gravity, where the axial
  gravitational perturbation couples to a dynamical scalar and the polar sector
  stays GR-like at the order considered, maps the perturbed Weyl scalar δΨ₀ onto
  the Zerilli–Moncrief function (even part) and the Regge–Wheeler function plus
  scalar radial perturbation (odd part). Derives the leading asymptotic h₊ and
  h× and finds no independent local term proportional to the scalar radial
  function in the leading tensor strain; offered as the formal basis for
  companion work on parity-split beating and effective ringdown frequencies.
  Perturbative analysis in one modified theory, no data; related in topic to the
  ringdown-amplitude entry in 2026-09-27. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27116

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Gravitational Path Integral and String Theory** — A. Sen, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (hep-th; gr-qc cross-list; 41-page review of mostly published
  results). Uses the supersymmetric index, one of the most controlled tests of
  the gravitational path integral, to argue that even there the naive integral
  cannot give consistent results beyond low orders; going further takes stringy
  inputs, namely the choice of UV cutoff and the correct integration variable,
  without a full string calculation. Also discusses how far one is led to a
  string-scale cutoff when starting from supergravity. Thematic neighbor of the
  supersymmetric-index entry in 2026-07-11. *tier: speculative_extension /
  review.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27073
- **Closed Universe, Quantum Entanglement and Observers** — H. Geng, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (hep-th; gr-qc, hep-ph cross-lists). Starts from the claim that a
  closed universe has a one-dimensional Hilbert space unless an "observer" is
  included, and shows that the quantum-circuit-holography model of the observer
  can be unified with the emergent-Goldstone-boson model arising from
  spontaneously broken diffeomorphisms. The author reads this as a manifestation
  of the "Massive Islands" paradigm, with the graviton mass a feature of the
  quantum state, islands as closed universes and the Goldstone boson as the
  observer, as at late times for evaporating black holes. Conceptual
  construction in the island literature seen in 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15.
  *tier: speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10667
- **Realization of de Sitter spacetime in AdS/CFT** — Y. Koga et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (hep-th; gr-qc cross-list). Builds horizonless, everywhere-regular
  solitons with a de Sitter core inside asymptotically AdS space, from Einstein
  gravity, a Maxwell field and a charged scalar: a gravitating gauged Q-ball
  whose false-vacuum interior acts as a positive cosmological constant, joined
  by a thin wall to a charged exterior that approaches the extremal
  Reissner–Nordström–AdS locus. Being static and spherical, it may correspond to
  a semiclassical charge-Q state of the dual CFT; the authors also discuss
  low-lying de Sitter-core modes, bulk reconstruction, and the near-extremal
  regime where finite-N effects are expected to cut off the classical throat.
  Hypothetical classical solution, no data. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27246
- **Probing the Black Hole Singularity with a Quantum String** — H. Singh and
  M. K. Nandy, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Places a Polyakov string in the
  Kantowski–Sachs black-hole interior, canonically quantizes the combined system
  into a Wheeler–DeWitt equation and solves it perturbatively to first order
  (Heun function for the angular part, Bessel for the geometric part at zeroth
  order). The wave function is regular at the singularity and the horizon;
  horizon regularity discretizes one eigenvalue and azimuthal periodicity
  quantizes another. Both orders satisfy the DeWitt boundary condition, which the
  authors read as resolving the singularity within this framework; a
  minisuperspace, perturbative model. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10861
