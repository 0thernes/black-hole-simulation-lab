## Shadows and disks

- **Naked Singularities and Black Hole Thermodynamics from Shadows and Photon Rings in the Quantum Oppenheimer-Snyder Model** — Jose Miguel Ladino, Anuar Idrissov, Carlos E. Romero-Figueroa. Submitted 2026-09-30. The abstract ray-traces thin-disk models in quantum Oppenheimer-Snyder spacetimes, both black-hole and naked-singularity cases, and says that raising the quantum-correction parameter reduces the apparent shadow size. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00640
  Why it matters here: shadow size is the quantity `tests/render_tests.cpp` checks against \(\sqrt{27}\,M\) in vacuum. A quantum correction that shrinks it is a different model and has to stay labeled.

- **Shadow signatures and near-horizon energy accumulation in coherent quantum sine-integral black holes** — A. Errehymy, Y. Khedif, M. Daoud. Submitted 2026-09-30. The abstract describes a sine-integral correction to the potential, set by a coherence scale \(R_s\), that softens the central region and can shift strong-field observables, including the shadow, away from classical GR. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08841
  Why it matters here: another shadow that is not Kerr. Same rule: not the default ray.

- **Thermodynamics, thermodynamic geometry, and the shadow of a Bardeen black hole surrounded by a Kiselev fluid: the role of regularity, asymptotics, and observational self-consistency** — Hoang Van Quyet. Submitted 2026-10-01. The abstract says the Bardeen–Kiselev solution is regular at the origin only for \(c=0\); for \(c\neq 0\) curvature invariants diverge as \(r\to 0\), and the spacetime is asymptotically de Sitter-like rather than flat. The shadow is part of the analysis. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03801
  Why it matters here: a reminder that a drawn shadow is only as good as the metric's regularity. The renderer assumes Kerr or Schwarzschild.

## Kerr, photons, and the metric the code trusts

- **Kerr stability in the full subextremal range** — Jérémie Szeftel. Submitted 2026-09-30. The abstract says the paper extends earlier proofs from the slowly rotating regime \(|a|\ll m\) to the full subextremal range \(|a|<m\), and that this completes the proof of the Kerr stability conjecture. That is the author's claim, recorded here, not a result this repository has checked. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40068
  Why it matters here: the spinning images assume a Kerr background. A stability theorem is context for that assumption. It is not a pixel test.

- **Axion corrections to photon superradiant scattering by Kerr black holes** — Zhi-Qing Zhu, Jun Zhang. Submitted 2026-09-30. The abstract solves the coupled axion and electromagnetic equations on Kerr and says the axion-photon coupling changes the electromagnetic amplification factor, in two regimes set by the axion mass and the sourced frequency. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40166
  Why it matters here: the ray tracer follows vacuum null geodesics. Superradiant scattering of an electromagnetic wave is a different calculation. It does not belong in the disk colour.
