## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Signatures of Black Hole Spin in Horizon-Scale Polarimetry** — G. N. Wong
  et al., 2026, *Galaxies*. Separates spin signatures coming from photon
  propagation in Kerr from those mediated by horizon-threading fields and
  magnetized plasma; concludes current EHT data constrain field geometry, flux
  state and orientation far better than spin magnitude or sense. *tier:
  observational_constraint / review.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/ad8da9096ba35603b008a5a42efc9fac/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Precision Ringdown Measurements of Binary Black Hole Remnants** — A. Kumar
  et al., 2026. RingCWB, a ringdown pipeline built on coherent WaveBurst,
  applied to GWTC-3; combined (2,2) constraints δf₂₂₀ = −0.005 ± 0.028 and
  δτ₂₂₀ = 0.032 (+0.108/−0.090), consistent with GR. *tier:
  observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/0e5095953c985b6cb40303bd8b3901d6/
- **Testing gravity with black hole ringdown amplitudes** — Anonymous, 2026,
  *Physical Review D*. Argues extra field-induced modes (dynamical Chern–Simons,
  Einstein-scalar-Gauss–Bonnet) are usually omitted from ringdown templates;
  including them tightens bounds and enables tests not based on QNM frequency
  shifts. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/6f48eb6e4963514abfb32e28d7ad1270/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Entanglement islands and information recovery from near-extremal regular
  black holes** — A. Anand et al., 2026. T-duality-inspired regular black hole
  with a minimal length; area term vanishes at extremality leaving a purely
  logarithmic remnant entropy; standard vs. thermodynamically corrected island
  functionals give different Page times and saturation values. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/2712b7ee8c8f5022b5e203180f00550d/
- **The Black Hole Information Paradox: Origins, Battles, and Where We Stand
  Now** — A. S. Babra, 2026, *IJSR*. Review tracing Bekenstein entropy →
  complementarity → firewalls → Page curve → islands → fuzzballs; also weighs
  Adami's 2025 stimulated-emission channel proposal. Low-tier venue; useful only
  as an orientation map. *tier: speculative_extension / review.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/7db6a5b9cbe35e49bef69f1ce9055daa/
- **Information surfaces and overflow fields: a unitary evolution model based on
  the principle of information curvature scaling** — Y.-H. Zheng, 2026,
  *EPJ Plus*. Credible-but-fringe: posits no interior singularity, an
  "information surface" plus overflow field, two-stage evaporation ending in a
  quantum outburst. Phenomenological, no derivation from a UV theory. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/653b3c697bed531785f5bd6c80aeba7b/
- **Replica Rényi Wormholes in JT Gravity and Modular Hamiltonians in Interface
  CFT** — L. C. Piper, 2026 (thesis). Extends the QES entropy formula to Rényi
  entropies and studies grey-body-factor effects on QES in JT gravity. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/dba4166bf02e52f5bba1afd4bd2c2e2a/
