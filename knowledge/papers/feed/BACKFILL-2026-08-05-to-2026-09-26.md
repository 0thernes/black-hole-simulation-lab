## Black-hole shadows, photon rings, thin disks

- **Optical-area minimum method for static spherical black hole shadows** — Vitalii Vertogradov, Nikko John Leo S. Lobos, Ali Ovgun. Submitted 2026-08-21. The abstract states an optical-area rule for static spherical shadows: for \(ds^{2}=-A(r)dt^{2}+B(r)dr^{2}+C(r)d\Omega^{2}\), a null ray of impact parameter \(b\) crosses a spherical section only if \(b^{2}\le C/A\), and the capture threshold is the infimum of \(C/A\). **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21018
  Why it matters here: that infimum is the critical impact parameter. For Schwarzschild this code fixes it at \(\sqrt{27}\,M\).

- **Deviations from Kerr: Polar critical curves and photon rings in a class of separable spacetimes** — Hao-Peng Yan, Xiang-Qian Li, Xiao-Jun Yue. Submitted 2026-09-01. The abstract gives an analytic description of polar critical curves and photon rings for a class of stationary axisymmetric metrics with separable null geodesics, set up so they can be compared with Kerr. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01290
  Why it matters here: the photon ring and the critical curve are the edge of the shadow image. A non-Kerr separable metric is a labeled variant, not a replacement for the tested Kerr tracer.

- **Polarization Images of Neutron Stars Illuminated by a Thin Accretion Disk: A Comparison with Black Holes** — Dan-Dan Feng, Guo-Ping Li, Chen-Yu Yang, Hong Lei. Submitted 2026-09-01. The abstract computes linear polarization images of static spherical neutron stars lit by a geometrically and optically thin disk, integrating null geodesics and parallel-transporting the polarization in the geometric-optics approximation, and compares them with black holes. **Backfilled 2026-11-10.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00934
  Why it matters here: the disk image here is total intensity only. Polarization is a later observable, and it needs the parallel transport this tracer does not do.
