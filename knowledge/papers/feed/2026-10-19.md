## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Probing Kerr spacetime via interferometric signatures of higher-order
  images** — F. Feleppa et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Analytic
  strong-deflection-limit description of the complex visibility of a finite-size
  compact source near a slowly rotating Kerr black hole, at linear order in
  spin. For a static equatorial source it gives a direct relation between the
  spin parameter and the angular displacement of successive higher-order images
  in the same parity sequence; for a source on a circular equatorial orbit,
  frame dragging and source motion shift the images in opposite angular
  directions, so the long-baseline visibility pattern reflects their
  competition. Slow-rotation analytic framework; no data comparison in the
  abstract. *tier: preprint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11391
- **Observational Signatures of Kerr Black Hole Lensing** — T. P. Kling et al.,
  2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.GA; arXiv comment says accepted to MNRAS).
  Numerically integrates strong-field null geodesics around rotating black
  holes and compares image locations and shapes of extended sources at
  different spin values. For the Galactic-centre black hole it reports that
  resolutions of order hundreds of μas are needed to separate the true from the
  lensed source position, and below 1 μas to determine the spin from lensing.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22470
- **The transformative power of ALMA2040 for mm-VLBI** — The Event Horizon
  Telescope Collaboration, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.IM; ALMA2040 white
  paper series, 16 pages). Simulated observations compare a larger phased ALMA
  with the Wideband Sensitivity Upgrade (WSU): sub-mJy baseline sensitivity at
  230 GHz in the configurations studied, better faint-source imaging at 345 GHz,
  improved multi-frequency recovery of M87* from the ring-like region to the
  jet base, and a 27% smaller error in the recovered intrinsic Sgr A* structure
  under scattering mitigation relative to WSU. Forecast for a future facility,
  not data. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22662
- **A model for the image structure and spectral energy distribution of
  relativistic jets** — D. W. Pesce et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE;
  submitted to ApJ per arXiv comment). Python implementation of the
  semi-analytic jet model of Tsunetoe et al. (2025): axisymmetric force-free jet
  plus nonthermal electron heating, synchrotron cooling, pitch-angle anisotropy
  and straight-ray radiative transfer. For M87 a single fiducial parameter set
  qualitatively reproduces multi-frequency VLBI images and the longitudinal
  intensity profile and gives a radio-through-X-ray SED consistent with
  observations; a fit to the quiescent Sgr A* SED is consistent with a compact
  jet whose radio size shrinks inversely with frequency. Model-fitting study,
  not a model-independent measurement. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20961

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Near-Extremal Black-Hole Formation Observed from Null Infinity** — T. Mädler
  et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Fully nonlinear, spherically
  symmetric Einstein–Maxwell–charged-scalar evolutions in 3+1 dimensions form
  black holes with charge-to-mass ratios up to 0.993 from regular, horizon-free,
  superextremal initial data. Quasinormal frequencies and redshift decay each
  independently recover the near-extremal ratio; critical mass scaling is
  recovered at null infinity and charge scaling at the apparent horizon; the
  charged-scalar Newman–Penrose quantity obeys an asymptotic balance law that
  replaces the neutral-theory conservation law. Spherical symmetry only.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23486
- **Collisions of Multiple Black Holes: A Numerical Relativity Study** — C.-K.
  Qiao and S.-Y. Liu, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Head-on collisions of
  several initially-at-rest Bowen–York black holes, evolved with BSSN in the
  AMSS-NCKU code. Perfectly symmetric configurations (vertices of a regular
  polygon or polyhedron) strongly constrain the emitted waves and give no
  gravitational kick; polygons are dominated by the plus polarization of the
  ℓ=2, m=0 mode, while for polyhedra the ℓ=2 modes nearly vanish and amplitudes
  drop to about 10⁻⁴ or below, in line with a quadrupole-approximation
  analysis; m≠0 radiation matters only for non-symmetric configurations.
  Idealized initial data, not an astrophysical scenario. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09336
- **Quasinormal modes of rotating black holes with ultralight scalar fields** —
  L. Lui and A. K.-W. Chung, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Reports the first
  accurate computation of leading-order QNM frequency shifts for rapidly
  rotating black holes surrounded by massive scalar fields with nonminimal
  curvature coupling. For reduced Compton wavelengths no shorter than the
  black-hole mass scale, numerical errors are typically 10⁻⁸–10⁻⁵ for spins 0 to
  0.9 and at most O(10⁻³) next to the scalar-mass threshold. The authors say
  this opens a route to searching for ultralight scalars in existing
  gravitational-wave signals; no such search is performed in the abstract.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16054
- **Towards ultrarelativistic scattering near the light ring: asymptotic
  divergence of gravitational-wave fluxes** — D. Trestini et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc; hep-th cross-list). For an ultrarelativistic particle near
  the scattering/capture threshold of a Schwarzschild black hole, at first
  order in the mass ratio, the fluxes radiated to infinity and absorbed by the
  horizon diverge as the inverse distance from the light ring times its
  logarithm. A WKB solution of the Regge–Wheeler equation gives the exact
  coefficient of the logarithmic enhancement, identical for both fluxes;
  subleading pole coefficients are estimated by combining low-multipole numerics
  with exact large-multipole asymptotics, and an extension to equatorial
  circular orbits in Kerr is conjectured. Thematic neighbor of the Barausse
  light-ring entry in 2026-10-16 (not a duplicate). *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20708

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Bound States Inside the Horizon and Black Hole Entropy** — H. Firouzjahi,
  2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc; hep-th cross-list). Counts discrete
  bound-state perturbation modes (labels s, p, ℓ, m) inside the Schwarzschild
  interior in the microcanonical ensemble. Requiring the shortest resolved
  wavelength to stay above the quantum-gravity scale imposes ℓ_max ~ r_H/L_Pl,
  so the degeneracy scales with horizon area and S = αA/ħG. Thermal equilibrium
  at the Hawking temperature fixes the bound-state energy fraction; for
  gravitational perturbations the contribution is typically small, and
  saturating the full entropy is possible in state counting but needs an
  order-unity energy fraction, so backreaction could not be ignored.
  Semiclassical counting argument, no observational content. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21426
- **Horizon-Scale Corrections to Black Hole Geometry from a Conserved Quantum
  Atmosphere** — K. A. Yasir, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc; physics.space-ph,
  quant-ph cross-lists). Adds a conserved Hawking-scale anisotropic source
  standing in for the coarse-grained near-horizon atmosphere and solves the
  Einstein equations for a static black hole at fixed ADM mass. The dressed
  geometry has a renormalized mass function, a shifted horizon, a modified
  surface gravity, an inward-moving ISCO and longer near-horizon residence
  time; offered as a deterministic mean-field base for later Einstein–Langevin
  treatments. Single-author work whose novelty claim is the author's own;
  credible-but-fringe. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07934
- **Finite-cutoff geometry from double-scaled SYK** — S. E. Aguilar-Gutierrez,
  2026, *arXiv preprint* (hep-th; gr-qc, quant-ph cross-lists). Builds
  deformations of Kourkoulou–Maldacena and thermofield-double SYK states whose
  double-scaling limits give TT̄-like deformations, describing wormhole states
  in finite-cutoff sine dilaton gravity and finite-cutoff AdS and dS geometries
  in the low- and high-energy regimes. Only the perturbative branch of the
  energy-spectrum flow admits physical quantum states; end-of-the-world branes
  give black-hole or eternal traversable-wormhole geometries depending on
  normalizable bound states, and constant-mean-curvature slice lengths are read
  as boundary complexity. Lower-dimensional toy holography. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20703
- **Pseudo-spectral methods in gravitational lensing: Spin precession in loop
  quantum black hole spacetimes** — X. Pang and X.-K. Guo, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Recasts lensing as a two-point boundary-value problem
  (fixed deflection angle, impact parameter as nonlinear eigenvalue) solved by
  Chebyshev collocation, with spin-precession parallel transport reducing to
  one linear system on the same grid. Applied to the covariant effective-metric
  and self-dual loop quantum black holes: quantum corrections are largest for
  slowly moving particles; in the ultra-relativistic limit the universal spin
  flip is recovered, but the rate of approach depends on the quantum parameters
  of both spacetimes. Model spacetimes, no data; the numerical method applies to
  any static spherically symmetric metric. *tier: speculative_extension /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18064
