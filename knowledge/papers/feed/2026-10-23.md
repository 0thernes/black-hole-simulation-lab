## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Photon-Return and Polar-Focusing Crossovers near Extremal Kerr** —
  V. Gonzalez-Moya et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). For equatorial
  prograde photons near extremal Kerr, derives a single scaling law for how
  finely the impact parameter must be tuned to complete n windings before
  escape, ε_n ≈ √3 κ/(e^{2πnκ}−1) with κ=√(1−a²/M²), bridging the extremal
  inverse-winding hierarchy and subextremal exponential accumulation. A second
  law for the polar Jacobi phase shows focusing points crossing from geometric
  to uniform spacing ΔN≃(2κ)⁻¹; both are checked against numerical
  integration of the exact Kerr equations (the authors' check). A candidate
  high-spin test case for the renderer's higher-order image structure.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21848
- **Modeling MAD Sgr A\* flares and their dependence on spin** —
  E. Antonopoulou et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE; arXiv comment
  says submitted). Tracks a flux eruption in magnetically arrested disk GRMHD
  runs over a range of spins and models the flare as a relativistic hot spot
  ejected along the flux tube; the tube wraps the jet funnel opposite to the
  spin, imprinting spin on the sky trajectory. Compared with the 2018 July 22
  GRAVITY flare, the authors favour large spin (over 90% of their flare models
  pass for a maximally rotating hole), with the inferred spin orientation tied
  to the assumed disk rotation sense. Model-dependent. *tier: preprint /
  observational_constraint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30095
- **Matter Induced Bounds: Photon Ring, Lyapunov Exponent, Induced
  Temperature** — H. Lee and B. Gwak, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc).
  Analytic bounds for rotating black holes with anisotropic matter: with a
  constant equatorial equation-of-state parameter, finite asymptotic mass and
  non-negative energy density, the equatorial photon ring radius cannot exceed
  the Kerr value at the same mass and spin (co- and counter-rotating); lower
  bounds are set at the outer horizon. The Lyapunov-exponent bounds constrain
  eikonal QNM damping, and a probe-string "induced temperature" gives
  2πT_ind ≥ λ. A possible sanity bound for non-Kerr metrics in the renderer.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30736

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Accurate initial data for Cauchy-characteristic evolution** — G. Da Re
  et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Traces the long-lived spurious
  transients in Cauchy-characteristic extraction of NR waveforms to incorrect
  initial angular coordinates, and builds initial data matching the worldtube
  data through second radial order with consistent angular coordinates. A 10%
  modulation of a BBH (2,2) amplitude lasting over 1000M disappears, and the
  late-time tail of a head-on collision is recovered; archived simulations can
  be reprocessed. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30268
- **Post-adiabatic effective-one-body waveforms for coalescing compact
  binaries** — R. Gamba, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Applies osculating-
  orbit (equinoctial-element) methods to eccentric, spinning, tidal and
  precessing TEOBResumS-Dalí dynamics. Over 10⁴ BNS and 10⁴ BBH systems with
  e₀ up to 0.6 at 20 Hz, median speedups are about 2.6 (BNS) and 1.5 (BBH)
  with mismatches against the full solution below 5×10⁻⁷ (the author's
  benchmark); also yields frequency-domain eccentric EOB waveforms. *tier:
  preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30064
- **Quasinormal-mode stability bounds in gravitational EFT: resolvability,
  predictivity, and positivity** — L. Juliano and E. Trincherini, 2026,
  *arXiv preprint* (hep-th). Argues that a proposed consistency bound (EFT
  corrections may lengthen a QNM decay time by at most about one period) gives
  no new model-independent constraint under single-scale power counting: the
  shift becomes order one only at ℓ∼(r_sΛ)², where the EFT stops being
  predictive, and any resolvable window coincides parametrically with
  flat-space positivity bounds, except possibly one family of dimension-ten
  scalar–tensor operators. A critique of a proposed ringdown test, not a
  measurement. *tier: preprint / speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28721
- **Exact algebraic separation of the gravitoelectromagnetic perturbations of
  Kerr-Newman-(anti-)de Sitter black holes** — J.-K. Zhao, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Separates the coupled gravitational and electromagnetic
  radiative equations on nonextremal, charged, rotating Kerr–Newman–(A)dS
  using a constant-determinant matrix independent of frequency and azimuthal
  number, shown smooth and invertible on the exterior, valid at arbitrary
  complex frequency, and related to Hintz's integrating factor in the flat
  limit. Groundwork for QNM and stability studies; no spectra computed here.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22274
- **Gravitational Wave Diffractive Images Reveal the Structure of
  Astrophysical Objects** — H. G. Choi et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*
  (astro-ph.CO). At linear order in the lens potential, relates the central
  diffractive image in a lensed GW waveform to the Fourier transform of the
  projected lens density (a point-mass Born amplitude times a mass form
  factor, as in nuclear scattering). With full wave-optics calculations the
  authors forecast that five years of next-generation detectors could
  separate extended lenses from point masses and halo-dressed from bare
  primordial black holes, and in close configurations detect scalar
  superradiance clouds for boson masses of roughly 10⁻¹⁷–10⁻¹² eV. Forecast
  only. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31441

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Quantum gravity on the bulk cone** — A. Almheiri et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (hep-th). Bulk-cone singularities in holographic thermal
  correlators, such as those from null geodesics winding the AdS photon sphere,
  are argued to be absent in CFTs dual to gravity at finite G_N, for both
  individual microstates and the thermal state, via analytic continuation in
  temperature. An AdS₃ two-shock geometry shows a bulk-cone singularity that an
  explicit CFT calculation smooths out at finite G_N, read as quantum fuzziness
  of the bulk. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31610
- **Quasinormal modes, complex null geodesics and singularity resolution in
  regular AdS black holes** — H.-Y. Chen et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*
  (hep-th). Uses WKB quasinormal modes of regular AdS black holes to read off
  dual CFT two-point functions: the singularity tied to a null geodesic
  bouncing off the AdS-Schwarzschild singularity becomes a smooth bump linked
  to a complex null geodesic, supported by OPE analysis. Proposed as a
  holographic signature of interior singularity resolution. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30915
- **The Stringy Photon Sphere** — A. Sheta, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (hep-th).
  Derives the lightcone singularities of the S-matrix on asymptotically flat
  Schwarzschild as a function of position on null infinity, including those
  from geodesics orbiting near the photon sphere, and shows finite string
  length moves them into the complex time plane. Also gives stringy
  corrections to the eikonal Schwarzschild QNM spectrum. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30426
- **Free fermionic black holes as polarised mirrors** — M. Krumtunger et al.,
  2026, *preprint* (venue not given by Consensus). Replaces the Haar-random
  unitary of the Hayden–Preskill "mirror" argument with a random free-fermion
  model: low-fermionic-degree information is retained until almost all of the
  black hole has evaporated while high-degree information is reflected fast (a
  "polarised mirror"). Adding a fixed Clifford unitary restores mirror
  behaviour, and in the high-fidelity limit recovering one qubit takes about
  37% fewer emitted qubits than the Haar case (the authors' result). A toy
  scrambling model, not a black-hole spacetime. *tier: preprint /
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/4076e562ac0156a4913e1d0306268349/
