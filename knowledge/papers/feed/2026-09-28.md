## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **A Brief Review of Spin Signatures in Time-Averaged Total Intensity Images of
  LLAGN Accretion Flows** — D. Palumbo, 2026, *Galaxies*. Splits spin
  signatures into coarse features that ground VLBI at 230/345 GHz can reach and
  fine photon-ring features that need space VLBI. Photon-ring observables are
  less sensitive to disk astrophysics, and the review projects ~±10% spin
  measurements for M87* and Sgr A*. *tier: observational_constraint / review.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/1bf6b866efe953e7937bed4263084824/
- **Observational Tests of Sgr A*'s State of the Accretion Flow** —
  F. Yusef-Zadeh et al., 2026. Simultaneous multi-wavelength campaign testing
  GRMHD MAD predictions; most are supported, jet evidence is unclear, and the
  field geometry looks like a hybrid: MAD-like field strength with SANE-like
  geometry. *tier: preprint / observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/93a6c180b30850b8a40f6c1e33b88f9c/
- **GRMHD Simulations of Magnetized Accretion Disk/Jet: Variabilities of Black
  Holes and Spectral Energy Distributions in Magnetic States** — R. Raha et al.,
  2026, *Universe*. 3D GRMHD at a = 0.998 across MAD / intermediate / SANE
  states; horizon magnetic flux sets jet efficiency and radiative output
  (MAD > INT > SANE); results are mapped onto GRS 1915+105, Cyg X-1 and HLX-1.
  *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/66540c16bff65e659ce2567a3b061c9c/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Ringdown analysis of GW250114 with orthonormal modes** — M. Suzuki et al.,
  2026, *Physical Review D*. Orthonormalized-QNM basis removes inter-mode
  correlations; support for the (2,2,1) overtone rises from 82.5% to 99.9%
  and no deviation from Kerr is found. *tier: observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/a4baa60efad753929e4657b380fff984/
- **Kerr Black Hole Ringdown in Effective Field Theory** — W. L. Boyce et al.,
  2026. Model-independent EFT corrections to Kerr QNMs at arbitrary spin; near
  extremality the corrections oscillate in log(T_H/Ω_H), a sign of discrete scale
  invariance. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/595a5859fccb5464b13868787103170b/
- **Nonlinear tails in the Kerr black hole ringdown** — S. Ling et al., 2026,
  *Physical Review D*. Extends second-order "sourced" power-law tails from
  Schwarzschild to Kerr using the Teukolsky equation; derives t^(−ℓ−β−s) and
  confirms it numerically. The power laws match Schwarzschild. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/2de82459c5ef5b6caeb14094558bf004/
- **Beyond black hole spectroscopy: Quasinormal mode contamination by massless
  scalars** — M. Y. Asuncion et al., 2026. In shift-symmetric Horndeski at
  O(q²), only the scalar–Gauss–Bonnet coupling produces both QNM frequency
  shifts and scalar-mode contamination. If scalar amplitude is not suppressed,
  contamination can dominate. *tier: preprint / speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/25f730cf4f67563b94a660917c6aec37/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Modular Krylov Complexity as a Boundary Probe of Area Operator and
  Entanglement Islands** — N. Vardian, 2026. Reconstructs the QES area
  operator purely from boundary modular dynamics (Lanczos coefficients plus
  OAQEC), and uses it to diagnose island formation and the Page transition
  without bulk extremization. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/fcf780132d495d609eb4fa8a11b10eb3/
- **Hawking Radiation from Tunneling in Black Hole Quantum Mechanics** —
  C.-S. Chu, 2026. Credible but fringe: models the black hole as a fuzzy sphere
  with a half-filled Fermi sea, with Hawking decay as monopole-mediated
  tunneling. Recovers Page's semiclassical rate and a Boltzmann spectrum, and
  unitarity is manifest. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/001e27d668b652408f51a9974891cc10/
- **Quantum optical inspired phenomenological models for unitary black hole
  evaporation** — P. M. Alsing, 2026, *Classical and Quantum Gravity*. Gaussian
  beam-splitter/squeezer toy model tracked symplectically. Aims at a
  near-thermal spectrum plus a Page curve and serves as an analog, not a
  derivation. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/5eb39c2cb9dd5103bf804e2ddaa27d11/
- **Hawking Emission from Black Holes Evaporating toward Wormholes and the
  Accuracy of the WKB Approximation** — B. C. Lütfüoğlu, 2026, *Physics
  Letters B*. Replaces WKB greybody factors with direct numerical scattering
  for Simpson–Visser and CFM geometries; WKB error reaches orders of magnitude
  near the cold endpoint (lifetime coefficient ×~85). This is a useful caution
  for any greybody code. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/5800234c0e8053478cf43281725118d1/
