## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Analytic solutions for the gravitational spin Hall equations and black hole
  shadows** — M. A. Oancea, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Beyond the
  geometric-optics limit, localized wave packets pick up frequency- and
  polarization-dependent corrections to null-geodesic motion. Integrates the
  gravitational spin Hall equations analytically to first order in wavelength
  in a large class of type D black-hole spacetimes (wherever the null
  geodesics themselves have analytic solutions) and obtains closed-form
  shadow shapes with polarization-dependent corrections. Relevant as a
  reference for what a pure null-geodesic ray tracer leaves out; the abstract
  gives no magnitude of the correction or comparison with data. *tier:
  preprint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08428
- **Polarimetry of Black Hole Spin and Axisymmetric Emission Geometry** — V.
  Bernshteyn et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE). Uses a semi-analytic
  conical emission model to ask what EHT-style polarimetric spiral measures
  (the β₂ coefficient) would constrain, for both the direct (n=0) image and the
  strongly lensed n=1 photon-ring image expected from the proposed Black Hole
  Explorer. For cone-aligned magnetic fields arg(β₂,n=0 β₂,n=1*) ≈ 4χ (χ the
  in-cone field orientation), while arg(β₂,n=0 β₂,n=1) suppresses magnetic
  geometry yet keeps a spin-direction dependence. Spin and cone opening angle
  are not tightly constrained by β₂ alone without strong priors on the field
  geometry. Model-based forecast, not a measurement. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03943
- **The Structure in the Structure Function of Black Hole Light Curves** — D. C.
  M. Palumbo et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.HE; arXiv comment says
  accepted in ApJL). Builds a heterogeneous Sgr A* 230 GHz light-curve set
  spanning about 12 years and studies its structure function analytically
  (Matérn-covariance processes), with lensed synthetic movies, and against a
  library of 72 GRMHD simulations. Magnetically arrested disk (MAD) models give
  smoother, steeper-sloped structure functions than SANE models; on 5–15 minute
  timescales the observed slope aligns more closely with MAD, while both model
  families are more variable than the data on all sub-hour timescales. *tier:
  preprint / observational_constraint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02729
- **Studying the Near-horizon Jet of M87 using Millimeter Balloon-borne VLBI
  stations** — F. M. Thiel et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (astro-ph.IM;
  submitted to ApJ per arXiv comment). Synthetic VLBI of M87 between a
  near-future EHT configuration and one or three 2.5 m-class balloon-borne
  stations on a transatlantic flight path, assuming 230/86 GHz frequency phase
  transfer. Adds baselines down to 0.2 Gλ (one balloon) and 0.07 Gλ (three);
  Bayesian imaging with Comrade.jl recovers jet structure up to 150 μas and
  300 μas respectively, with jet-image NRMSE improved by factors of about 1.2
  and 1.3 over EHT-only. Simulation study of a proposed array, not data.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19204

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Gravitational radiation from a photon on the light ring: multipole fluxes
  and the logarithmic divergence** — E. Barausse, 2026, *arXiv preprint*
  (gr-qc). A massless particle on the circular null geodesic of a Schwarzschild
  or Kerr black hole radiates at every multipole ℓ with power falling only as
  1/ℓ, so the total instantaneous power diverges logarithmically; derived
  analytically from the Zerilli–Regge–Wheeler and Teukolsky equations, with a
  closed-form spin factor g(a) in terms of the Kerr light-ring radius, and half
  the radiation falling into the hole at leading order. Cause is near-resonance
  with the quasinormal modes plus a source cancellation specific to massless
  particles; massive particles near the light ring get an exponential cutoff.
  Checked numerically up to ℓ=12800 (Schwarzschild) and ℓ=800 (Kerr). The
  author also reports that the odd-parity term of the 1973 Breuer–Ruffini–
  Tiomno–Vishveshwara synchrotron formulae is too small by a factor of four.
  Companion code repository linked in the arXiv comment. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12082
- **Black Hole Fine Structure** — A. K.-W. Chung et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*
  (gr-qc). Theory-agnostic search of gravitational-wave observations for
  breaking of isospectrality (identical axial and polar QNM spectra in GR),
  which new physics could turn into a fine-structure-like spectral splitting.
  Finds no evidence of splitting and places a combined 90% upper limit of
  <28.5% on the relative frequency splitting; mapped onto several
  modified-gravity theories this bounds coupling lengths to ≲28 km. *tier:
  preprint / observational_constraint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18989
- **Quasinormal-Mode Collider Spectroscopy** — M. Zhu et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (gr-qc). Framework (borrowing from cosmological collider physics)
  for organizing and predicting nonlinear black-hole ringdown. Two structural
  results: at an isolated nondegenerate intermediate pole the cubic strain
  residue factorizes into an excitation amplitude times a quadratic conversion
  coefficient, imposing rank constraints that can test the intermediate-mode
  description; and the pole residue can be reconstructed from horizon and
  infinity radiation. Thematic neighbor of the cubic-QNM entry in 2026-10-15
  (not a duplicate). No waveform comparison in the abstract. *tier: preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04595
- **Binary Ellis-Bronnikov wormholes in 3+1 numerical relativity: mergers,
  fly-bys and gravitational waves** — N. M. Shirokov and I. Nachevsky, 2026,
  *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Claims the first 3D numerical-relativity evolutions
  of wormhole binaries, held open by a ghost scalar field. Throat e-fold time
  matches the linear rate to within 1.9%; like scalar charges repel and opposite
  charges attract (attraction-to-repulsion ratio 1.462 ± 0.022 versus the
  predicted 3/2), so only opposite-signed pairs merge; a head-on collision forms
  a common marginally outer trapped surface that then loses 28% of its area
  swallowing the phantom field. The authors conclude these binaries have no
  inspiral. Exotic-matter model, not an astrophysical-black-hole prediction;
  code and data on Zenodo per the arXiv comment. *tier: speculative_extension /
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13206

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **The Forgetful Black Hole: Memory Burden and Hysteresis in Hawking
  Evaporation** — Y. C. Ong, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Revisits the
  memory-burden proposal that Hawking emission is increasingly suppressed as
  stored quantum information becomes costly to maintain. Derives a conditional
  no-go: entropy-suppressed, zero-mean deposits with subextensive integrated
  correlations cannot yield an order-one luminosity change through unamplified
  accumulation and bounded feedback. Dynamical memory needs a record that is
  written, retained and coupled back; toy models then give either a prolonged
  finite lifetime or an effective remnant, plus evaporation–accretion
  hysteresis. Toy-model reasoning, no data comparison. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18997
- **Reflected entropy and islands in JT gravity from partial dimensional
  reduction** — D. Basu and H. Chourasiya, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (hep-th).
  Computes reflected entropy for adjacent and disjoint radiation regions in
  Jackiw–Teitelboim gravity coupled to a non-gravitating bath, in the vacuum
  and the thermofield-double state of an eternal black hole. Reflected entropy
  resolves entanglement-wedge structure the ordinary Page curve cannot: its
  dominant saddle can change while the union's entropy saddle stays fixed, and
  it vanishes at leading order when the wedge disconnects. The reflected-island
  and higher-dimensional defect-extremal-surface pictures agree to leading
  order in the brane fluctuation. Two-dimensional toy gravity. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13900
- **Quantum covariant bit threads** — M. Headrick et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*
  (hep-th). Derives prescriptions equivalent to the quantum extremal surface
  formula but phrased as minimal or maximal bulk flows (spacelike V-flows and
  timelike U-flows), with flow lines allowed to end at bulk points according to
  bulk entanglement entropies. The quantum extremal surface appears as a
  bottleneck: some threads pass through (the area term) and others jump across
  (the entropy term). Offered as a complementary view on evaporation, islands
  and baby universes. Formal holography, no observational content. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03929
- **Spontaneous Hawking Radiation from Dynamical Black-Hole Formation in
  Quantum Spin Systems** — S. Kinoshita et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*
  (hep-th). Shows Hawking-type particle creation arising spontaneously, from the
  early-time vacuum with no prepared excitations, in a local quantum spin model
  evolving under a dynamical black-hole-forming geometry. A local spin observable
  reproduces the continuum renormalized energy density, including its
  dependence on surface gravity, over a time window that grows logarithmically
  with system size before lattice-cutoff effects take over. An analogue
  quantum-simulation route, not a gravitating black hole. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation (analogue model).* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07794
