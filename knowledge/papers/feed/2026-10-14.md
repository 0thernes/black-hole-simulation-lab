## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **CoportSL: A Contribution-constrained Hybrid Slow-light Framework for
  Time-dependent Polarized GRMHD Imaging** — F. Zhou et al., 2026, *preprint*.
  Fast-light imaging neglects fluid evolution along rays, while full slow-light
  full-Stokes transfer needs many GRMHD snapshots in memory. CoportSL uses
  emission, absorption and Faraday contributions to keep slow-light only where
  evolution matters. On M87*-like MAD data, normalized Stokes differences stay
  below 4×10⁻³ with 44.7–75.3% fewer snapshot layers, and fast light is reported
  to miss substantial full-Stokes structure. A relevant reference for the
  fast-light vs slow-light approximation in our renderer. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/412e8a2a32845193a8fe5e9d00f9525b/
- **Polarization Signatures of Sgr A\* with Tilted Hybrid Magnetic Fields** —
  Y.-S. Feng et al., 2026, *preprint*. A split-monopole field plus a tilted
  uniform external field, run through a semi-analytic RIAF with GR polarized
  radiative transfer. The tilt changes the EVPA pattern and brightness
  distribution and makes the polarization morphology depend on the observer's
  azimuth, which axisymmetric fields do not do. The paper compares the result
  with EHT data to assess whether such hybrid fields are plausible; the model is
  stationary and semi-analytic. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/b2dbb6c7e2265b4abd6018e96d47eb03/
- **Kerr-Degenerate Shadows and Distinct Strong-Deflection Lensing in Rotating
  Hayward-like and Bardeen-like Geometries** — C.-H. Hsiao et al., 2026,
  *preprint*. For a common Kerr-like ansatz, the complete photon family gives a
  shadow boundary identical to Kerr at the same mass, spin and inclination, so
  EHT-based shadow diameters give no extra constraint on the regularity length.
  The strong-deflection limit is only partly degenerate: image separation,
  relative magnitude and the subleading time delay depend on the regularity
  length. Useful as a caution that shadow size alone cannot distinguish these
  metrics from Kerr. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/0fd1b82b46aa5c4e86766bce8c5d3823/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Constraining the Quadratic-mode Amplitude Coupling in GW250114** — Y.-X.
  Yang et al., 2026, *preprint*. Bayesian inference on the GW250114 ringdown,
  modeled with the 220, 221 and 220×220 modes, infers the amplitude-coupling
  coefficient of the quadratic mode. It is consistent with numerical-relativity
  fits and second-order perturbation theory, with no comparison deviating by
  more than 1.3σ. The current data cannot separate the spin-dependent
  predictions. *tier: preprint / observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/af5fa0181edb5b2b93924b08c4147bc0/
- **NRSur7dq4v2: A multi-domain precessing surrogate model with improved
  accuracy** — A. Ravishankar et al., 2026, *preprint*. A multi-domain extension
  of NRSur7dq4 with separate error control for inspiral and merger-ringdown,
  modes up to ℓ=5, and a user-selectable accuracy/cost setting. Remnant mass and
  spin from quasi-normal fits to the surrogate ringdown improve by roughly 3 to
  10× over v1. The paper also reports a fourfold gwsurrogate speedup for
  precessing surrogates. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/71c9b12619df595fa9cddfb4cdacde00/
- **Dissection of a merger-ringdown waveform in the small-mass-ratio limit** —
  P. Bourg et al., 2026, *preprint*. A framework for isolating nonlinear
  merger-ringdown features for a quasicircular, nonspinning binary. The waveform
  splits into an extended inspiral, a roughly half-cycle merger, and a QNM-
  dominated ringdown. Quadratic QNMs dominate linear overtones in the ringdown
  for comparable-to-intermediate mass ratios, and memory effects are discussed.
  *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/25711c3938fc59ddad5fc5b8641e4862/
- **Parameter Estimation of Ringdown Quasinormal Modes with Autoencoder** — M.
  Iida et al., 2026, *preprint*. An autoencoder for multi-component QNM
  denoising and parameter estimation, tested on synthetic sums of Kerr QNMs built
  from recent high-precision frequencies and excitation factors. It recovers the
  two longest-lived components of eight-component waveforms in-domain, and
  degrades when validation spins lie far outside the training range. Only
  controlled model waveforms are tested, so this is a method demonstration, not
  a data result. *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation (method).*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/05ec6d4feb3050aab77c041f7bb2a558/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Non-Perturbative Transitions in the Island Formula: A Finite-N Resolution of
  the Page Curve Kink** — S. Saha, 2026, *Physics Letters B* (venue as listed by
  the index). In JT gravity with a holographic CFT bath, the paper derives a
  leading finite-N correction δS ∼ O(e^(−S_BH)) from Hawking/replica-wormhole
  interference, which replaces the sharp Page-time kink with a log-sum-exp
  crossover. It is checked against a random-unitary-circuit simulation and a
  matrix-model truncation, and claims information begins to leak before the Page
  time. The novelty claims are the author's own and unreviewed here. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/5412c2c4104552e8a548793fc15a8502/
- **Reconstruction Support and Island Dominance in Black-Hole Information
  Recovery** — H.-F. Ding, 2026, *Quantum Reports*. Separates three logically
  distinct statements: island-saddle dominance, existence of a radiation recovery
  channel, and the cost of implementing it. Islands and the Page transition come
  from the standard generalized-entropy/QES prescription, then a model-dependent
  reconstruction score ranks QES candidates. The paper states that the score is
  not a new gravitational entropy formula. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/6473e809144759278cfeaa76a9cfb7e0/
- **Unitary evolution of an evaporating black hole in canonical quantum
  gravity** — S.-J. Wang et al., 2026, *preprint*. Models evaporation without an
  event horizon, assuming each time slice is a coherent state in gravitational
  phase space with nonzero overlap between spatial configurations. A toy model is
  semiclassical at the coarse-grained level and unitary at the fine-grained
  level. The stated cost is a violation of the entropy bound. *tier:
  speculative_extension (fringe-but-credible).*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/41bf208c938e5e59be152c201d10227f/
