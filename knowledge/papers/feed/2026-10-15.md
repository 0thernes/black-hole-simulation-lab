## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Testing a quantum motivated effective black hole geometry with photon ring
  and orbital diagnostics** — K. Ghaderi et al., 2026, *Physics Letters B*. A
  one-parameter static spherical deformation of Schwarzschild with an r⁻³ term,
  treated as an effective template and not a unique microscopic prediction.
  Derives the horizon, photon sphere, critical impact parameter, ISCO,
  radiative efficiency and epicyclic frequencies. Mapping fiducial 68% EHT
  Sgr A* shadow-size intervals gives −1 ≤ χ ≤ 0.863 (Keck calibration) and
  −1 ≤ χ ≤ 0.156 (VLTI calibration). Under Planck-suppressed matching the
  geometric ISCO shift is negligible next to the Schwarzschild self-force for
  astrophysical extreme-mass-ratio systems. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/277777d677a255df90923be01e777c2a/
- **Optical appearance of cosmological black holes in f(R) gravity** — J.
  Sultana, 2026, *Physics Letters B*. Builds analytic approximations of H(z) and
  the angular-diameter distance in popular f(R) models, then computes an
  approximate shadow size for a spherically symmetric black hole seen by a
  distant comoving observer. Compared with the ΛCDM result of Bisnovatyi-Kogan
  and Tsupko, with differences from GR discussed against EHT observations.
  Context for cosmological-background corrections to shadow size, not a
  Kerr-spin result. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/3cc9180bba3b5fa0845d24f1259504b1/
- **Chromatic photon-ring spectroscopy of asymmetric thin-shell wormholes in a
  dispersive plasma** — A. Övgün et al., 2026, *Physics Letters B*. Two
  unequal-mass Schwarzschild exteriors joined at a throat, in cold
  non-magnetized plasma; derives the plasma-modified radial potential, critical
  impact parameters and disk transfer functions. With equal plasma parameters
  the model is a self-similar control with identical fractional chromatic
  dependence on both sides, so differential ring motion needs differing plasma
  strengths or falloff indices; the authors present it as a conditional,
  plasma-model-dependent diagnostic. The displayed intensity is a finite-order
  (m ≤ 4) bolometric proxy, not order-converged multifrequency radiative
  transfer. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/6293d80c91715b98b4b17905f863a6f9/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Cubic Quasinormal Modes in the Black-Hole Ringdown** — N. Khera and H.
  Yang, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Formulates and numerically solves the
  nonlinear Teukolsky equations through third order for Kerr, using
  lower-order metric reconstruction in split light-cone gauges and a
  hyperboloidal compactified formulation on complex radial contours.
  Spin-dependent coupling coefficients fix cubic amplitudes from products of
  linear parent amplitudes. Applied to one nonprecessing NR waveform, the cubic
  terms improve agreement over selected early-ringdown fitting windows and
  shift inferred overtone amplitudes without extra fit parameters; 3M after the
  |h₂₂| peak the summed cubic term reaches about 38% of h₂₂. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20547
- **Accounting for detector calibration uncertainty in black-hole ringdown
  analysis** — R. S. Nathan et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Two
  QNM-rational-filter approaches: apply a best-estimated calibration
  correction, or jointly sample remnant mass, spin and a frequency-dependent
  calibration error under an uncertainty prior. On SXS:BBH:0305 injected into
  colored noise (SNR ≈60, ±25% magnitude and ±25° phase errors), marginalizing
  recovers the true mass and spin within the 90% credible interval while
  ignoring the error biases them. Applied to GW250207, where the Hanford
  response was not well characterized, the ringdown-only remnant is consistent
  with the inspiral-merger-ringdown prediction. *tier: preprint /
  numerical_approximation (method).* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11052
- **Ringdown analysis of GW250114 with RingCWB** — A. Kumar et al., 2026,
  *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Measures the dominant (2,2,0) mode with its first
  overtone and constrains fractional deviations δf₂₂₀ = 0.004 (−0.023/+0.022)
  and δτ₂₂₀ = 0.010 (−0.087/+0.096), both consistent with zero, plus a tight
  remnant-spin constraint consistent with stellar-evolution formation. *tier:
  preprint / observational_constraint.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09503
- **Biases in Ringdown Tests of General Relativity from Gravitational-Wave
  Microlensing** — S. Mundakkal Dasan et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc).
  Simulated GW150914-like signals across long-wavelength, wave-optics and
  geometric-optics lensing regimes, analysed with unlensed waveform models.
  Wave-optics lensing can bias the inferred remnant mass and spin and
  spuriously favor a first overtone that was not injected; the abstract
  reports Bayes-factor values of about 40 for that overtone and about 8 for
  apparent deviations from the Kerr QNM spectrum (notation as in the abstract).
  Evidence for higher-order modes is largely unchanged. Simulation study only.
  *tier: preprint / numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20201
- **A catalog of long, eccentric and precessing binary black hole simulations
  from ICTS-TIFR** — V. Prasad et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). 31 SpEC
  simulations targeting the sparsely covered eccentric-and-precessing corner:
  17 are both, with mass ratios 1 ≤ q ≤ 11 and initial eccentricity up to 0.40;
  the longest runs 80 orbits (159 GW cycles). Compile-time optimisation for
  AMD Zen-2 gave a 90% speedup of the initial-data solver and 40% of the
  evolution, at 1.18 × 10⁶ CPU-hours total. The first law of BBH mechanics
  holds to better than 0.54% for eω < 0.2, and the co-precessing
  approximation's mirror-symmetry violation sits 3 to 4 orders above the
  numerical floor (0.7% to 5.3% with opening angle). *tier:
  numerical_approximation.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19688
- **Listening to the Horizon: Probing Near-Horizon Reflectivity with GW250114**
  — R. Prasad et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint* (gr-qc). Uses the prompt
  non-QNM "direct wave" of GW250114 as the normalization for a null test of
  horizon reflectivity: a partially reflecting exotic compact object would
  return a delayed direct-wave echo with amplitude set by |R_s|. No echo is
  found (B = −2.9), giving |R_s| < 0.06 (90% credible), a reflected energy
  fraction below 0.4%, for surfaces from 0.1 M_f to a Planck length from the
  would-be horizon. The authors note it is unclear whether the direct wave
  itself carries horizon information, and the bound depends on the echo
  template. *tier: preprint / observational_constraint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12429

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Where in the Island is the Information?** — T. Banks, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint* (hep-th, gr-qc). Takes the island formula for quantum extremal
  surfaces of evaporating large AdS black holes and the apparent paradox that
  the island is entangled with radiation across regions causally disconnected
  since long before the Page time. Gives two arguments that the island's
  information sits in a tiny spacelike region very near the horizon at the
  Page time: covariant-entropy-bound limits on causal diamonds just before the
  Page time, and a tensor-network RG argument that a typical microcanonical
  pure state concentrates on a narrow range of radii. Conceptual, not a
  calculation checked against data. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02406
- **Reconstruction of Black Hole Islands from Hawking Radiation via the
  Algebraic Petz Map** — N. Vardian, 2026, *arXiv preprint* (hep-th, gr-qc,
  quant-ph). Constructs an island-reconstruction protocol with the algebraic
  Petz map: modular theory reflects island operators into the accessible
  complement, transports them to the boundary and maps them into the radiation
  algebra. The derivation is said to yield the structure of Papadodimas-Raju
  mirror operators. The claim that this proves information inside the black
  hole can be decoded is the author's own and unreviewed here. *tier:
  speculative_extension.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10769
